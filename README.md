# 📊 Shopora Business Intelligence & Sales Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Shopora's sales, profitability, customer behaviour, and delivery performance using **Microsoft Power BI**. The goal is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards, data modelling, and analytical reporting.

The project explores key business questions to identify sales trends, evaluate product profitability, understand customer purchasing patterns, and uncover opportunities for business growth and operational improvement.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit performance across product categories and subcategories.
- Identify monthly revenue trends and potential seasonal patterns.
- Evaluate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Investigate product returns and delivery performance.
- Understand customer segments and repeat purchasing behaviour.
- Compare regional revenue performance and identify growth opportunities.
- Highlight loss-making product subcategories and areas for improvement.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and business metrics.
- **Data Modelling** – Establishing relationships between Orders, Order_Items, Customers, and Products tables.
- **Microsoft PowerPoint** – Presenting key findings and recommendations.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The Power BI report includes interactive dashboard pages covering:

- **Executive Dashboard:** Overview of key business performance indicators.
- **Sales Performance:** Revenue trends, monthly performance, and sales analysis.
- **Product Performance:** Product category profitability and subcategory comparisons.
- **Customer Performance:** Customer segmentation, purchasing behaviour, and customer value.
- **Delivery Performance:** Delivery delays, delivery performance, and returns-related analysis.
- **Regional Performance:** Revenue comparisons and regional trends.
- **Detailed Analysis:** Additional breakdowns to support deeper investigation.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The analysis highlights several important findings:

- **Product profitability:** Phones, Bags, and Accessories are among the strongest profit contributors, while Laptops show a significant loss.
- **Revenue trends:** November and December show notable revenue peaks, although monthly performance varies between years.
- **Discount analysis:** Higher discounts do not consistently translate into higher profits.
- **Delivery performance:** Late deliveries represent a substantial share of orders and warrant operational attention.
- **Customer segmentation:** Premium customers contribute the highest revenue and profit among the customer segments shown.
- **Regional performance:** The South shows strong revenue growth, while Central experiences a decline between the two years compared.
